D&D 5.5e DM/Reference Screen

+ Core Rules Reference (Rolls, Cover, Vision, Death)

Standard Rolls

  • To Hit: d20 + Ability Mod + (Prof. Bonus)
  • Tool/Skill: d20 + Ability Mod + (Prof. Bonus)
  • Melee Damage: Die + Weapon Ability Mod
  • Ranged Damage: Die + Weapon Ability Mod
  • Spell Save DC: 8 + Ability Mod + Prof. Bonus

Cover & Stealth

  • Half Cover: +2 bonus to AC and Dex Saves
  • 3/4 Cover: +5 bonus to AC and Dex Saves
  • Total Cover: Cannot be targeted directly
  • Hide Action: DC 15 Stealth (Check vs Passive Percep)
  • Invisible: Adv. on attacks / Disadv. for attackers

Athletics (Jumping)

  • Long Jump: Up to Strength Score (feet)
  • High Jump: 3 + Strength Mod (feet)
  • Running Start: Requires 10ft movement first
  • Standing Jump: Half distance (Long or High)
  • Difficult Terrain: 1 extra ft movement per ft moved

Vision & Light

  • Bright Light: Normal vision.
  • Dim Light: Area is Lightly Obscured.
  • Darkness: Area is Heavily Obscured.
  • Lightly Obscured: Disadv. on WIS (Perception).
  • Heavily Obscured: Effectively Blinded.
  • Blinded: Auto-fail sight. Disadv on attacks.

Death & Stabilizing

  • Death Save: 10+ Success; 9- Failure.
  • Critical Success (20): Regain 1 HP.
  • Critical Failure (1): Counts as 2 failures.
  • Stabilizing: DC 10 Medicine check.
  • Stable: 0 HP; regains 1 HP in 1d4 hours.
  • Instant Death: Massive dmg exceeds HP max.
+ Precise Minion Table (CR 1/2 - 30)

Grouping: Up to five minions that share a turn may each contribute their actions to a Group Attack against the same target, making a single attack roll.
To Hit: Add +1 for each minion contributing to the attack beyond the first.
Damage: The group attack's damage is multiplied by the number of attackers contributing to it.
